
# Bill of Materials (BOM)

## Three-Wheel Differential-Drive AGV Prototype

### 1. Electronic Components

| No. | Component | Specification | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Arduino Uno | ATmega328P Microcontroller Board | 1 |
| 2 | Motor Driver | L298N Dual H-Bridge | 1 |
| 3 | DC Geared Motor | TT Motor, 3–6V | 2 |
| 4 | Ultrasonic Sensor | HC-SR04 | 1 |
| 5 | AA Battery | 1.5V Dry Cell | 4 |
| 6 | Battery Holder | 4-cell AA Battery Holder | 1 |
| 7 | Power Switch | Main Power Switch | 1 |
| 8 | Jumper Wires | Connecting Wires | As required |

### 2. Mechanical Components

| No. | Component | Description | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Chassis | AGV Frame | 1 |
| 2 | Drive Wheels | Motor-driven Wheels | 2 |
| 3 | Passive Wheel | Caster / Support Wheel | 1 |
| 4 | Motor Mounts | Motor Mounting Brackets | 2 |
| 5 | Fasteners | Screws, Nuts and Spacers | As required |

### 3. System Configuration

- Vehicle Type: Three-Wheel AGV.
- Drive Configuration: Differential Drive.
- Drive System: Two Independently Driven Wheels.
- Support System: One Passive Wheel.
- Main Controller: Arduino Uno.
- Motor Driver: L298N.
- Obstacle Detection: HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor.

### 4. Notes

- Verify all component specifications before assembly.
- Check motor polarity and electrical connections.
- Ensure sufficient power supply for both motors.
- Test the motor driver and ultrasonic sensor before operation.

### 5. Project Status

The three-wheel AGV prototype has been successfully assembled and is operational.

Basic motor control and vehicle movement have been implemented and tested.
